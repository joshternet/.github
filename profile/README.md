# The Joshternet

**Open standards and infrastructure for connecting Joshes across the web.**

> **Network status:** PRE-JOSH

The Joshternet is an open experiment in connecting people who identify as Josh, and the personal websites they call home.

The goal is intentionally simple: make it easier for Joshes to discover other Joshes, visit their websites, and wander through a small corner of the open web built by Joshes themselves.

We favor boring, open web standards, decentralized participation, and infrastructure that works without requiring a social network, follower count, ranking algorithm, or centralized identity provider.

## Standards

The Joshternet standards will be developed openly. The first specifications will define Josh identity, Josh discovery, and inter-Josh navigation.

**Joshness is declared, never derived.**

## Current status

The network is currently being defined.

No Josh nodes are operational yet.
